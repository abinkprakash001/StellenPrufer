About StellenPrüfer

Verify Before You Trust, A job and employer verification tool built for job seekers in Germany.

Why This Project Was Created

When applying for jobs in Germany, many applicants—especially students, graduates, and international job seekers—run into two major problems: fake job scams that try to steal personal data or money, and inactive “ghost jobs” (Scheinstellen) that stay online for months even though the company is no longer hiring.

StellenPrüfer was created to give applicants a fast, reliable way to check the real facts behind a job advertisement before they send their CV, passport, or personal details.

The Core Idea

Most fake job ads copy professional wording from real companies, so simply reading the text is not enough. Instead of guessing from text alone, StellenPrüfer cross-checks verifiable facts: whether the company is officially registered in Germany, whether its website and career page are active, how old the job posting is, and whether the offered pay and contract follow German labor laws.

You can either paste a job link or upload a contract on our website, or use our Chrome Extension to check jobs in one click directly while browsing LinkedIn, StepStone, Indeed, or Arbeitsagentur.

What We Check

  • Company Registration: Checks if the employer is officially listed in the German Commercial Register (Handelsregister HRB/HRA) or is a public institution.
  • Website & Email Safety: Verifies the official company domain, scans links for security threats (VirusTotal), and checks if the recruiter uses a real corporate email rather than free webmail.
  • Ghost Job & Vacancy Status: Warns if a job posting is already closed (“No longer accepting applications”) or has been online for more than 45 days.
  • Salary & Minimum Wage: Checks if the offered pay meets Germany’s statutory minimum wage (13,90 €/h under MiLoG) and realistic market rates.
  • Scam Words & Employment Contracts: Flags upfront payment demands, WhatsApp/Telegram-only interviews, and checks German employment contracts (Arbeitsvertrag) for missing legal clauses.
  • Resume Match: Compares the job requirements against your uploaded PDF CV so you can see how well your skills match the role.

How the Trust Score (0 - 100) Works

  • 70 - 100 (High Trust): Company registration, website security, and active vacancy checks passed. Safe to apply.
  • 45 - 69 (Medium Trust): Verify details before applying—either some contact details could not be confirmed, or the job posting is closed / older than 45 days.
  • 0 - 44 (Low Trust): High scam risk—warning signs such as upfront fees, unverified domains, or illegal wages were detected.
